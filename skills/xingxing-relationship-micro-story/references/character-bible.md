## Lock and variation matrix

**Always locked:** the two identities, adult age, relative height, hair, body proportions, relationship tone, indigo/oatmeal palette, natural anatomy, editorial watercolor treatment.

**Allowed to vary:** setting, action, props, camera distance, pose, gaze direction, time of day, weather, restrained expression, and one orange caring detail.

**Never vary:** ethnicity, age category, facial identity, couple pairing, illustration medium, or mascot identity. Do not use face-obscuring angles in more than one scene. Avoid identical poses across episodes.

## Continuity QA

Compare all five scenes with the anchor at full size and contact-sheet size. Fail the episode if either person changes face, age, hair length, height relationship, coat palette, or if hands/anatomy distract from the caring action. A scene that cannot be repaired is regenerated from the same anchor, not accepted as a new character.
