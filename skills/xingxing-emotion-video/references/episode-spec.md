# Episode v5 and DailyContentRun v2

New Hook-test work is generated by `emotion-card daily-program`; do not hand-author a disconnected episode.

## DailyContentRun v2

Required identity:

```json
{
  "schemaVersion": 2,
  "kind": "daily-content-run",
  "programId": "xingxing-daily-hook-copy-v2",
  "experimentId": "hook-copy-20s-002",
  "runDate": "2026-07-26",
  "pair": {
    "pairId": "hook-copy-20s-002-pair-01",
    "arm": "A",
    "separationHours": 48
  },
  "schedule": {
    "plannedStartAt": "2026-07-26T10:17:00+08:00",
    "scheduledFor": "2026-07-26T20:30:00+08:00",
    "scheduleCutoff": "2026-07-26T19:30:00+08:00"
  }
}
```

The run also stores:

- six topic scores and the selection reason;
- Hook type, concrete anchor, and exactly two-line 12–18-character Hook;
- `fixedTreatment` plus its SHA-256 fingerprint;
- five canonical action IDs and their actual image identities;
- scene moods, motion events, sound cues, and ratio-specific covers;
- video, BGM, cover, and publish-pack hashes;
- per-platform account, variant, remote schedule/work reference, evidence, and 2/24/72-hour checkpoints.

A/B counterparts must share an identical fixed-treatment fingerprint and differ in Hook text. Their platform schedule timestamps are exactly 48 hours apart.

## Episode v5

The five scenes are exactly:

1. `hook` — `xingxing_female`: concrete quote, behavior, time, or count;
2. `emotion` — `xingxing_female`: receive the feeling without diagnosis or attack;
3. `observe` — `xingxing`: one observable behavior;
4. `clarity` — `xingxing`: one sentence the viewer can say aloud;
5. `heal` — `duo`: shared outcome, with `engagementPrompt` overlaid.

Each subtitle line is at most 12 Chinese characters. Each scene contains `actionReason`, `sceneMood`, `roleLabel`, and planned motion/sound metadata.

`contentContext` includes:

```text
strategyId, runId, dailyContentRunId, topicId, pillar,
experimentId, variantId, controlSetId, programId, pairId,
fixedTreatmentFingerprint, hookOnlyTreatment
```

## Visual rules

- Use canonical IDs from `action-library.md`; aliases are legacy-read-only.
- All three roles appear and the five source images are visually distinct.
- `pass_star` and `shared_star` are one approved pixel identity and cannot count as a change.
- Compared with the previous topic, at least 3/5 positions change; the first two positions must not both repeat.
- No latest-10 identical image sequence.
- The B arm may intentionally reuse the A arm's five images only when its pair ID and fixed-treatment fingerprint match.
- Frame 0 is full color; 0–1.2 seconds loses no more than 20% color coverage. Later transitions never show a complete sketch or blank reset.

## Covers and audio

`coverPlan.headlineByRatio` contains `9:16`, `3:4`, `4:3`, `16:9`, and `1:1`. Each headline is 8–14 Chinese characters, at most two lines, phrase-safe, and fixed within the pair. Use canonical character art directly.

Post-cutover Hook-phase audio uses Xiaoyi `+0% / -2Hz`, the same complete BGM
without ducking, no opening chime, AAC 48 kHz, and audio/video duration
difference at most 50 ms. Keep the voice treatment identical across A/B.
`opening-only` means no additional observation/judgment semantic chimes.
