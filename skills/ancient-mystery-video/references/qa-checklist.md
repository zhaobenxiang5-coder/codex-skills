# QA checklist

Automated requirements:

- 10-14 formal images; no blanks or placeholders.
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC, yuv420p.
- 60-90 seconds.
- Research validation passes.
- Every scene has image, voice, SRT, duration, fact level, and source linkage where applicable.

Visual review:

- Opening, midpoint, and ending are visibly different.
- No image contains gibberish, logos, watermark, modern anachronism, extra limbs, or obvious visual corruption.
- Subtitle is at most two lines and inside the safe area.
- Key text is short; it does not cover the focal subject.
- Camera movement is subtle, not a slideshow zoom gimmick.
- Narration is clear; background music supports rather than masks speech.
- Evidence and disputed/speculative claims use the correct on-screen badge.
