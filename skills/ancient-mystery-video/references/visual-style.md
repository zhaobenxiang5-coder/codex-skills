# Visual style

- 1080x1920, 9:16 cinematic ancient-history reconstruction.
- Palette: dark gold, earth yellow, bronze green, limestone gray, charcoal black.
- No text, letters, numerals, captions, logos, UI, or watermarks inside generated images.
- Strong depth with foreground artifacts, middle-ground subject, atmospheric background.
- Plausible Chinese archaeology and architecture; avoid fantasy armor and modern props unless the scene is explicitly a modern excavation.
- Keep a consistent filmic grade and realistic materials across all scenes.
- Remotion supplies slow push/pan, vignette, dust, light scan, labels, subtitles, and transitions. Do not ask the image model to draw motion graphics.
